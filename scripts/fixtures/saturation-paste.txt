What's actually broken

Customers use it because we ship it for free with the platform, then immediately re-skin it (badly) before going to production. We lose a meaningful amount of perceived product quality at exactly the moment our customers are evangelizing us to their leadership.
- The dashboard is a similar story. Functional but visually inert. New users land, blink, and bounce. Our top-of-funnel CVR is ~4% — we believe a better dashboard pushes that to 8-10%.
